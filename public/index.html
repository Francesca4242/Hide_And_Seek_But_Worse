THE DEPARTMENT OF HIDE & SEEK

A Sub-Committee of the Ministry of Games, Pastimes & Related Paperwork

Founded in the wake of the Great Wardrobe Incident, the Department exists to ensure that no game of hide and seek is ever allowed to remain simple. Where children once hid behind curtains on a whim, they now do so under licence, in triplicate, with an Estimated Time of Discovery on file. We call this progress.

Seekers are issued a redacted Freedom of Information response in place of a plain list of names, because plain lists of names set a dangerous precedent. Hiders who are found may, if they believe the finding to be procedurally unsound, lodge a formal appeal before an impromptu Tribunal of their peers. Findings upheld in error cannot be unfound; this is, we are told, how bureaucracy works.

Motto: Quaerite et Invenietis, Eventualiter — "Seek, and Ye Shall Find. Eventually. Pending Review."
