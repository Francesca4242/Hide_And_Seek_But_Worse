Tribunal of Peers

From the Departmental Archive — the free-ish encyclopedia

Tribunal of Peers
JurisdictionContested findings only
CompositionAny player not party to the dispute
Standard of proofMajority vote, no appeal beyond appeal
Sessions heldAs needed, which is often

The Tribunal of Peers is the body convened whenever a hider disputes having been found, typically on the grounds that the seeker was guessing, exceeded their jurisdiction, or is simply mistaken about who they are looking at. Any player not directly involved in the finding is eligible, and in practice obligated, to serve.

Proceedings are brief by design: the round clock is paused for their duration, on the theory that justice should not be rushed but the game very much should be. A vote is cast, a verdict rendered, and — per longstanding tradition established shortly after the Great Wardrobe Incident — a chaos event is triggered the moment an appeal is lodged, regardless of how the Tribunal ultimately rules. The Department describes this as "keeping deliberations lively" rather than "an obvious structural flaw."

A hider holding a Pre-Approved Alibi Chit may bypass the Tribunal entirely by presenting it on the spot, a privilege the Tribunal has grumbled about but never formally contested.
