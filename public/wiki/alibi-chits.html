Pre-Approved Alibi Chits

From the Departmental Archive — the free-ish encyclopedia

Pre-Approved Alibi Chit
Issued byThe Department, grudgingly
Earned viaFiling Form 27B; passing a Compliance Audit
Redemption valueOne (1) guaranteed dismissal
TransferableNo
ExpiresEnd of round

A Pre-Approved Alibi Chit is a small unit of bureaucratic goodwill awarded to hiders who complete their paperwork correctly and, on occasion, correctly recall having done so. A hider holding at least one chit may, upon being found and challenged, present it in lieu of a defense before the Tribunal of Peers, resulting in an immediate and unappealable dismissal of the finding.

Chits are earned in two ways: filing Form 27B in the first place, and subsequently answering a Compliance Audit correctly when the Department, without warning, asks a hider to recall exactly what they wrote on it. The Department maintains that this system rewards honesty. Cynics maintain that it rewards having a good memory, which is a different thing entirely.

Chits do not carry over between rounds, cannot be gifted, borrowed, or pooled, and — despite being called "pre-approved" — are subject to being spent at the worst possible moment, usually by accident.
